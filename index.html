A note on reading online
02 / A little perspective

Context without the pile-on.

Curiosity is natural. Online attention can also spill into real life, especially when rumors invite people to search for or approach someone. Keep discussion focused on public material and avoid sharing private details or unverified claims.

CHECK

Separate reporting from rumor

Look for dates, original sources, and independent confirmation. Treat reposts and anonymous claims cautiously.

RESPECT

Give people privacy

Don’t seek out private addresses, relatives, or live locations. A meme is not an invitation to confront anyone.

CHOOSE

Skip graphic material

You can understand the cultural context without watching or recirculating disturbing content.
